## 👋 About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Developer and Data Analyst who enjoys owning a problem end-to-end — from architecture to deployment to the client call. I run **[Nuvirexa](https://nuvirexa.tech)**, a freelance web & AI development agency, alongside a part-time Data Analyst role and a final year of BCA (Hons) at Parul University, Vadodara.

- 🏗️ Recently shipped **FinFlow Analytics**, a real-time financial SaaS with WebSocket-driven dashboards and RBAC, deployed on AWS via Terraform
- 💼 Manage the full project lifecycle at Nuvirexa — scoping, development, client communication, and deployment

- 🎓 Graduating 2027, planning an MCA next
- 📍 Based in Vadodara, Gujarat — open to remote-first teams
